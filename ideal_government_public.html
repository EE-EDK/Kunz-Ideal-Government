01CONSTITUTION
ResolvedIn progressNot startedFlagged
C1

Additive-only amendment

3/4 coordination body + 75% popular supermajority to add. Never subtractive.

Resolved
C2

Natural rights recognized

Rights recognized, not granted. Source-agnostic: deism, Christianity, secular reason.

Resolved
C3

NAP as default norm

Non-Aggression Principle is the baseline behavioral standard. Voluntarism first.

Resolved
C4

10th/9th analog

All unlisted powers default explicitly to cells. No implied federal authority.

Resolved
C5

No international authority

System sovereignty is absolute. International cooperation is transactional, not binding.

Resolved
C6

Speech — radical, NAP-bounded

Maximum freedom of speech. NAP is the only limit. Cells handle nuance.

Resolved
C7

Religion — NAP-tested

Subsumed under speech. Voluntary practice protected. Governance application that violates NAP = void.

Resolved
C8

Arms — tiered

Household issue baseline. Personal through Class III constitutional. Cell militia for heavy. WMD equally distributed.

Resolved
C9

Travel — citizen floor

Search/seizure protections apply everywhere. Cell-licensed nuance (driving, etc.).

Resolved
C10

V+1 criminal standard

Retributive proportionality plus one degree for violent crime. Massive deterrent architecture.

Resolved
C11

Execution — tiered delegation

Victim → volunteer pool → automated chamber. Randomized actuation, fire cleanup. No permanent executor class.

Resolved
C12

National security body

Transparently chartered. Subsumes FBI/CIA/NSA. No domestic surveillance authority.

Resolved
C13

Oversight body

Elected separately. Dual: internal accountability + diplomatic interface. Hard-scoped.

Resolved
C14

Immigration — tiered

Foreign nationals: zero constitutional standing. Body floor + cell acceptance. Arduous citizenship path.

Resolved
C15

Resource contribution system

Value-percentage based. 10-year review cycle. No default income tax.

Resolved
C16

Parity number system

Technocratic, sector-owned, publicly transparent. All businesses require parity number at founding.

Resolved
C17

Progressive wealth equation

Significant wealth permitted. Takeover-scale accumulation structurally prevented via ceiling.

Resolved
C18

Birth accounts

Body floor + cell additive. Funded from cell value percentages. No citizen starts at zero.

Resolved
C19

No mandatory welfare

Voluntary support system + birth accounts replaces mandatory welfare.

Resolved
C20

Early adulthood petition

12+ with 75% judicial panel + mandatory parental inclusion. Rare genius cases can access agency early.

Resolved
C21

Cell absorption rules

Competitive incentive market. 90% dual supermajority. Accumulation ceiling enforced.

Resolved
C22

Cell disbandment — prohibited

Cells cannot be dissolved. Only absorbed with dual supermajority.

Resolved
C23

New cell formation process

Body-wide formal process. Resource allocation review triggered on instantiation.

Resolved
C24

WMD equal distribution

Capability-defined (10k+ casualties or 1+ sq mile). Equal custody across cells. Cross-cell manning.

Resolved
C25

Inter-cell movement

Citizen-preferenced process. Merit/achievement transfer pathway. Body citizenship portable.

Resolved
